Atomic Habits

Atomic Habits teaches that tiny daily improvements create massive long-term results. James Clear explains that habits work like compound interest for self-growth. The book focuses on building good habits and breaking bad ones through small changes. It highlights that systems are more important than goals. If you fix your routines, success naturally follows. Clear introduces the Four Laws of Behavior Change as a practical guide. These laws make habits obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying. The book shows how identity shapes behavior and how behavior shapes identity. By changing who you believe you are, habits stick more easily.
