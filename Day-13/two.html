Atomic Habits

  • Tiny changes compound into big results
  • Focus on systems, not just goals
  • Use habit stacking to build routines
  • Make good habits obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying
  • Design your environment to support behaviour

Atomic Habits by James Clear teaches that small, consistent improvements lead to remarkable results over time. Rather than relying on motivation or willpower, the book explains how to design simple systems and environments that make good habits automatic and bad habits harder to follow.
